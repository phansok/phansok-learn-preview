2. Conceptual / Theoretical Model

A Holistic Model of HIV, Aging, and Health

This framework brings together biological, psychological, social, and structural factors to understand the interconnected experiences of people living with HIV.

The model considers relationships among HIV and aging, health-related quality of life, stigma, depression, comorbidities, basic living needs, and other determinants of health. Measurement and prevention are also considered through MOS-HIV and PrEP.
